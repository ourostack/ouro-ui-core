# Decision: density moves the room, and fixed sizes read their own tokens

Status: accepted, 2026-11-21, for [ourostack/ouro-ui#417](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/417). Web builds it first.

## Why

An app that offers tighter or roomier spacing sets Tailwind's `--spacing`, the one base every spacing utility multiplies. Web's components also build fixed dimensions from that base (`size-8`, `w-*`, `h-*` on avatars, icons, rails and thumbs), so a density change resized them, and apps pinned them back by hand.

## Decision

- **Density is `--spacing`.** Gaps and padding follow it, and so do the heights of controls and rows (Button, TextField, Select, list rows, tabs), as compact density means in Spectrum and Material.
- **Floors.** On a coarse pointer a control's hit area stays at least 44 px. On a fine pointer a control is never shorter than its icon plus 4 px.
- **Fixed sizes read `--ouro-size-*`.** Icons, avatars, rails, sidebars, thumbs and switch tracks take their dimensions from `--ouro-size-*` variables in rem, defined in web's `ouro-base` and named the same in core's tokens, so the native kits map them. `--spacing` never touches them, and an app may theme them.
- **Checks in web.** Every story renders at `--spacing` 0.2 rem and 0.3 rem: fixed dimensions hold while gaps move. A source rule fails a spacing-scale dimension class on a fixed-size part. ConversationList at compact density keeps its avatars' size while its rows tighten.

## Prior art

Read on 2026-11-21.

| Find | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Material: applying density](https://m2.material.io/design/layout/applying-density.html), [Material 3: density](https://m3.material.io/foundations/layout/understanding-layout/density) | **Borrow** | Density steps take height off components and leave icons their size. Ouro takes the split; its knob is `--spacing` rather than numbered steps. |
| [Spectrum: platform scale](https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/platform-scale/), [TableView's density](https://react-spectrum.adobe.com/react-spectrum/TableView.html) | **Borrow** | Spectrum keeps two knobs: scale resizes everything, density changes padding and row height but never icons. Ouro's density is Spectrum's density. |
| [Fluent 2: layout](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/layout) | **Adopt** | Spacing and size are separate token sets. Ouro's `--ouro-size-*` beside `--spacing` is that split. |
| [Tailwind CSS: theme variables](https://tailwindcss.com/docs/theme) | **Adopt** | `--spacing` is the base apps already set, so density needs no new knob. |
| [Radix Themes: Theme](https://www.radix-ui.com/themes/docs/components/theme) | **Ignore** | Its `scaling` resizes everything, the opposite of what density asks for. |
